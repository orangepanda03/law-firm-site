Firearm & Weapons Offences

In Canada, firearm offences are prosecuted with absolute zero tolerance. Crown Attorneys and police forces across Toronto and Ontario treat gun charges as top-priority cases. Whether you are charged with unauthorized possession of a firearm, carrying a concealed weapon, or trafficking firearms, a conviction almost certainly results in a lengthy penitentiary sentence. To avoid catastrophic consequences, you need a highly strategic firearms lawyer.

Defending Against Gun Charges in Ontario

Firearms cases are highly technical. To secure a conviction, the Crown must prove beyond a reasonable doubt not only that a firearm was present, but that it was legally defined as a firearm, that the search was lawful, and that you had actual possession of it. A robust defence strategy involves:

  • Constructive Possession: If a gun is found in a shared vehicle, apartment, or backpack, the Crown must prove you had both knowledge of the weapon and control over it. Mere proximity is not enough to convict.
  • Charter Applications & Unlawful Searches: Police often discover weapons during traffic stops, street checks, or home raids. If the police violated your Section 8 Charter rights against unreasonable search and seizure, the firearm can be excluded from evidence, effectively destroying the Crown's case.
  • Cross-Examination on Fabrication: Exposing inconsistencies, collusion, or outright fabrication by witnesses or complainants who claim you possessed or pointed a firearm.
