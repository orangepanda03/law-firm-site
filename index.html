Trevor Lau

Criminal Defence Lawyer

Trevor Lau obtained his law degree from the University of Oxford, and a Masters of Law from Osgoode Hall Law School.

He is an experienced criminal defence lawyer who has successfully defended clients in both the Ontario Court of Justice and the Superior Court of Justice, against charges ranging from Impaired operation (DUI) to Murder. Trevor has also won appeal cases, which were featured in the Toronto Star.

Trevor defends clients across Ontario rigorously, and believes every client deserves honesty and communication throughout the criminal justice process. His approach combines aggressive advocacy with transparent client relationships.

Recent Successes

Proven track record of achieving favorable outcomes for clients facing serious criminal charges

R. v. C.G. (2026)
Charges: Assault, Fail to comply
Charges were withdrawn following negotiations with the Crown, wherein I showed the allegations were manifestly false. Client's immigration status was protected as a result.
No jail, no criminal record
R. v. C.S. (2026)
Charges: Sexual assault x2, Assault w/ Choking, Assault
Client acquitted of all charges after trial. Thorough cross-examination of the complainant revealed significant credibility and reliability issues in the Crown's case.
No jail, no criminal record
R. v. J.W. (2025)
Charges: Assault causing bodily harm & Assault
Convictions successfully overturned on appeal, after arguing that there was a reasonable apprehension that the trial judge had engaged in results-driven reasoning.
New trial ordered, charges later withdrawn. No jail, no criminal record
R. v. M.C. (2025)
Charges: Uttering threats to cause death
Following negotiations with the Crown, charges were withdrawn.
No jail, no criminal record
R. v. S.G. (2025)
Charges: Failure to stop after accident
Client risked being barred from obtaining PR status if convicted of a criminal offence. Following negotiations with the Crown, the case was resolved by way of a plea to Careless driving causing bodily harm under the Highway Traffic Act.
No jail, no criminal record
R. v. T.C.K. & R. v. A.M. (2025)
Charges: Possession for trafficking (Cocaine & Fentanyl)
Both clients had charges withdrawn after extensive pressure on the Crown to release disclosure.
No jail, no criminal records
R. v. T.L. (2025)
Charges: DUI, Sexual assault and Mischief Over
Client faced deportation if convicted. Sexual assault & Mischief Over were stayed (dropped) mid-trial. DUI resolved through a plea to Careless driving under the Highway Traffic Act.
No jail, no criminal record
R. v. A.M. (2024)

No jail, no criminal record
