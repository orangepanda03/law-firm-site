Domestic Assault

Police forces and Crown Attorneys in Ontario have a strict zero-tolerance policy for domestic violence. If the police are called to a domestic disturbance and have reasonable grounds to believe an assault occurred, they must lay a charge. Furthermore, it is a myth that the complainant can simply "drop the charges"—only the Crown has that authority. If you are facing domestic charges, you need an experienced domestic assault lawyer to protect your rights, your family, and your freedom.

Navigating the Complexities of Domestic Allegations

A domestic assault charge immediately fractures a family. You will likely face restrictive "no-contact" bail conditions, forcing you out of your home and away from your children. A strategic defence aims to mitigate these immediate hardships while systematically dismantling the Crown's case. Key strategies often include:

  • Bail Variations: Applying to the court to modify restrictive no-contact orders, allowing you to communicate with your partner or see your children while awaiting trial.
  • Peace Bonds: Negotiating aggressively with the Crown to have the criminal charges withdrawn in exchange for entering into a Section 810 Peace Bond, ensuring you avoid a criminal record.
  • Exposing False Motives: Domestic assault allegations are sometimes exaggerated or entirely fabricated to gain leverage in contentious family law disputes or divorce proceedings. We rigorously cross-examine complainants to expose these ulterior motives.
  • Self-Defence: Demonstrating that physical contact was a lawful and proportionate response to a threat of violence against yourself.
