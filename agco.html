Hearings Before the License Appeal Tribunal (LAT)

Individuals or businesses who dispute a Notice of Proposal, an Order of Monetary Penalty, or other decisions from the AGCO can appeal to the LAT. Hearings before the LAT require meticulous preparation and an aggressive defensive execution. Depending on the nature of the state's allegations, your case will proceed via one of two formats:

Hearing Format Procedural Mechanics The Legal Strategy
Oral / In-Person Hearings
  • Conducted live or via interactive video before a Tribunal panel.
  • Involves formal opening and closing submissions.
  • Requires the formal presentation of evidence and exhibits.
  • Trevor executes intensive examination and cross-examination of witnesses.
  • Systematically breaks down the credibility and reliability of state inspectors.
  • Delivers sharp, high-impact oral submissions directly to the panel.
Written Hearings
  • Conducted completely through asynchronous written media.
  • Eliminates live testimony.
  • Decided entirely based on the submission of formal records.
  • Trevor drafts thorough and nuanced written submissions/factums.
  • Structures a comprehensive matrix of case precedents and documentary evidence.
  • Neutralizes inspector allegations without allowing live biases to color the record.
