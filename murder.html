Being investigated or charged with a violent crime—ranging from aggravated assault to homicide—is the most terrifying experience a person can endure. Murder charges carry the absolute harshest penalties under the Criminal Code, including mandatory life imprisonment. Cases of this magnitude are prosecuted by the Crown's most experienced trial lawyers and investigated by elite police homicide and major crime units. To level the playing field, you need a formidable murder and violent crimes lawyer fighting for your life.

Defending Against Homicide & Serious Assault

Defending a murder or severe violent crime charge requires an exhaustive review of forensic evidence, witness credibility, and police procedure. An airtight defence strategy is custom-built for the facts of your case and may involve:

  • Self-Defence: Establishing that your actions were a legally justified response to a reasonable apprehension of death or grievous bodily harm to yourself or another person.
  • Lack of Intent (Mens Rea): For a murder conviction, the Crown must prove specific intent. We rigorously challenge the evidence of intent, which can be the difference between a murder conviction, a reduction to manslaughter, or a complete acquittal.
  • Identity & Forensic Flaws: Scrutinizing the reliability of eyewitness testimony, CCTV footage, and forensic evidence (like DNA or blood spatter) to introduce reasonable doubt regarding the identity of the perpetrator.
  • Charter Violations: Filing applications to exclude confessions or statements obtained through oppressive police interrogations or unlawful detentions.
