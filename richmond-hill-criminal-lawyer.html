Richmond Hill & York Region Criminal Defence Lawyer

Trevor Lau obtained his law degree from the University of Oxford, and a Masters of Law from Osgoode Hall Law School.

With a convenient York Region office located at 120 East Beaver Creek Rd in Richmond Hill, Trevor defends clients facing charges initiated by York Regional Police (YRP) and the OPP. All criminal matters arising in Richmond Hill, Markham, Vaughan, and Aurora are prosecuted at the Newmarket Courthouse (50 Eagle St. W.), where Trevor regularly appears in both the Ontario Court of Justice and the Superior Court of Justice.

Whether you are facing allegations of domestic assault, impaired driving (DUI), sexual assault, or complex indictable offences, Trevor combines aggressive courtroom advocacy with transparent, honest client communication.

Trevor is a member of the Criminal Lawyers' Association, the Canadian Bar Association, and the Ontario Bar Association.
